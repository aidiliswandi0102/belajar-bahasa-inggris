Deep Dive

Simple Present — Full Guide

📖 What is Simple Present?

Used for habits, general truths, jobs, and feelings.

  • Habits/Routines: "She goes to school every day."
  • General Facts: "The Earth revolves around the Sun."
  • Jobs: "He works as a teacher."
  • Feelings: "I like coffee."

🔧 Formula

Positive Subject + Verb (+s/+es) + Object

✓ She plays tennis.

Negative Subject + do/does NOT + Verb

✗ She does not play tennis.

Question Do/Does + Subject + Verb?

? Does she play tennis?

👤 Do vs Does

SubjectHelperExample
I / You / We / They do I play football.
He / She / It does He plays football.

💡 When to add -es

Ends in -s, -x, -z, -ch, -sh

pass → passes watch → watches

Consonant + y

study → studies carry → carries

Ends in -o

go → goes do → does

Vowel + y (just add -s)

play → plays stay → stays

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ She don't like pizza.

✓ She doesn't like pizza.

❌ He go to school.

✓ He goes to school.

❌ Do she like coffee?

✓ Does she like coffee?

❌ They doesn't play tennis.

✓ They don't play tennis.
